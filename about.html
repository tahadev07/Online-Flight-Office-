Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below to view quick answers about bookings, baggage, and cancellations.

How quickly will I receive my e-ticket after registering?

Once your booking request is confirmed, our office issues an official airline e-ticket with a verifiable 6-character PNR code within 15 to 30 minutes via email.

Can I modify my departure date after purchasing a ticket?

Yes. Economy Flex, Business, and First Class tickets allow date modifications. Contact our 24/7 desk at least 24 hours before departure to adjust your itinerary.

What passport validity is required for international flights?

Most international destinations require your passport to be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned return date. Please double-check your passport number during sign-up.

Do you offer special discounts for university students?

Yes! Students receive discounted fares and extra baggage allowance on selected routes to Europe, Turkey, and the Gulf when registering with a valid student ID.
